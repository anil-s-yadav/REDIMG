1. Overview

Welcome to REDIMG ("we", "our", or "us"). We provide utilities to resize images to target KB sizes, create passport photos, generate digital signatures, compress photos, combine signatures with photos, and convert images into PDF documents.

We take user privacy with the utmost seriousness. This Privacy Policy informs you about how our application handles device storage, image data, and permissions in accordance with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement and User Data policies.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike many online converter tools, REDIMG is built as an on-device utility:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, or physical locations.
  • No Image Uploads: Photos, cropped passport images, signatures, and converted PDFs are processed locally via on-device code. They are never transmitted to any remote cloud server.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: We do not include third-party tracking SDKs (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Facebook Pixel, Mixpanel, or Crashlytics tracking personal behavior).
  • No Advertisements / Tracking IDs: We do not display ad network banners or access the Google Advertising ID (AAID).

To perform user-selected file operations, REDIMG requests only the minimum necessary Android permissions:

4. Data Storage & User Control

All exported files (such as .jpg, .png, or .pdf) are saved directly in your device's public Downloads / RedImage directory.

  • You retain 100% ownership and control over your files.
  • You can delete, share, or move your saved files at any time via your device's built-in File Manager or the "My Files" screen.
  • Temporary scratch files created during image processing are automatically cleaned up upon task completion.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is a general-purpose image and PDF utility suitable for all audiences. Because we do not collect, transmit, or monetize any personal data, our application complies fully with COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act) and the EU GDPR children’s privacy directives.
